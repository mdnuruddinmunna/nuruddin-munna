What a podcast edit covers

Multicam

Multicam episode edit

Every camera and the audio synced, then cut to whoever's talking, with reaction shots where they add something and the dead air taken out.

Audio

Clean, even sound

Noise and room echo cleaned up, filler and false starts removed, levels matched between voices and the whole episode mastered to a steady loudness.

Remote

Remote interviews

Zoom and Riverside recordings laid out side by side or stacked, with name lower thirds so viewers always know who is talking.

About

Three years in the edit, most of it in other people's voices.

I've cut video agency-side as creative head at Aves Digital, for an Australian solar and battery brand at Electrifying Australia, and at Quantanite. I also work directly with brands, agencies and creators across four countries.

Premiere Pro for the cut, After Effects for everything that moves on top of it. What I care about is retention: the cold open that stops a thumb, the trims nobody notices, the caption that lands one frame before the word.
